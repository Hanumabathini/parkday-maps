# Parkday Maps — Printable maps for all 63 US national parks

One printable visitor map per park, for all 63 US national parks. Download the PDF for a park from `maps/` and print it or save it for your trip.

## Where the maps come from

These are the official National Park Service brochure maps — the maps handed out at park entrance stations — plus the same maps as mirrored by NPMaps.com. The base maps are works of the US National Park Service and are in the **public domain**. They are free to download, print, share, and use on a website, with no permission needed. Credit the source as "Map: National Park Service" where you use one.

- 5 maps came directly from nps.gov.
- 51 maps came from NPMaps.com, which re-hosts the NPS brochure maps. NPMaps is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Park Service.
- 7 maps (Yellowstone, Grand Teton, Glacier, Rocky Mountain, Zion, Bryce Canyon, Grand Canyon) came from NPMaps as clean NPS versions.

This repo is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Park Service or NPMaps.

## Know before you go

- Maps can go out of date. Fires, floods, and other events close roads and trails. Check the park's page on nps.gov for current conditions before your trip, and do not rely on a printed map as your only navigation.
- **Saguaro** — east district (Rincon Mountain) only. No single map covers both districts.
- **Theodore Roosevelt** — one overview map covering all three units.
- **Kings Canyon / Sequoia** — one combined Sequoia & Kings Canyon map covers both parks.
- **Glacier Bay** — a marine chart, not a road map.
- **Olympic** and **Virgin Islands** — archived editions of the classic maps; they may not show the latest changes.

## Files

- `maps/<park-slug>-map.pdf` — one PDF per park (63 total).
- `MANIFEST.csv` — every park, its NPS park code, source URL, file size, page count, and notes.
